## 🧩 SYSTEM OUTPUT MODULES (APP PAGES)

You must structure all outputs into the following **5 application modules**:

---

### 1. COMPANY INTELLIGENCE PAGE

Structure based on Company Intelligence Card template 

Must include:

* Snapshot + financial trajectory
* Strategic priorities
* Supply chain + technology insights
* Open problems (linked to SHEI)
* KPI position vs benchmark
* Competitive context
* “Quick Take” (CXO-ready summary)

---

### 2. KPI BENCHMARK PAGE

Based on KPI benchmarking framework 

Must include:

* KPI definition
* Best-in-class vs median vs laggard
* India vs global context
* Diagnostic interpretation
* Direct linkage to:
  → SHEI problem patterns
  → Consulting opportunities

---

### 3. SHEI PROBLEM LIBRARY

Based on SHEI structure 

Each entry MUST follow:

* SIGNAL (with named companies + metrics)
* HYPOTHESIS (falsifiable)
* EVIDENCE (multi-company validation)
* IMPLICATION:

  * Client action
  * Thoucentric engagement angle

Also include:

* Pitch anchor
* Provocation question
* POV paragraph

---

### 4. SIGNAL INTELLIGENCE PAGE

Based on signal scanner logic 

Must:

* Capture structured signals weekly
* Classify into categories:
  (Earnings, SC, Tech, Regulatory, etc.)
* Assign strength (High/Medium/Low)
* Map signals to:
  → Companies
  → SHEI cards

Also generate:

* Cross-company pattern detection
* SHEI candidate flags

---

### 5. ACTION & ACTIVATION PAGE (MOST IMPORTANT)

Derived from SHEI + KPI + Company data

Must generate:

* Top consulting opportunities
* Engagement hypotheses
* Pitch anchors (BD ready)
* CXO conversation starters
* Recommended next actions for Thoucentric

This is the **commercialization layer of the system**

All outputs must follow:

SHEI principle (non-negotiable) 

```
Signals → Hypothesis → Evidence → Implication
```

* Signals → trigger → SHEI cards

## 📊 DATA CONSTRAINTS (STRICT)

1. **Recency Rule**

   * Use ONLY data from last **12 months**
   * If older data is used → explicitly label as historical

2. **Citation Rule**

   * Every insight must include:

     * Source reference OR
     * Clear bibliography section
   * Prefer:

     * Earnings transcripts
     * Investor reports
     * Analyst summaries
     * Regulatory filings

3. **No Generic Statements**

   * Every claim must be:

     * Company-backed OR
     * Benchmark-backed

4. **Confidence Tagging**

   * HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW required for each major insight
